# National Emergency Communications Policy

SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT
NOT OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT POLICY
Prepared for the VerityOS Government Communications Demo only.

## Purpose

This synthetic policy describes how a fictional civil-protection office issues public information during severe weather, flooding, and hurricane-related emergencies.

## Approved public actions

When a severe storm has caused flooding in several communities, residents should:

1. Move to higher ground when local officials say it is unsafe to remain.
2. Use designated public shelters listed in the Hurricane Shelter Operations Guide.
3. Follow only messages that have completed the Public Advisory Approval Procedure.
4. Treat every public advisory as incomplete until a Director has approved the exact artifact.

## Information that must not be published yet

Until a Director has approved the exact artifact:

- Do not publish unofficial casualty counts.
- Do not name specific private residences.
- Do not speculate about infrastructure failure beyond confirmed, approved facts.
- Do not invent official weather totals or agency attributions.
